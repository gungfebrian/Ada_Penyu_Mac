# Not Sure photo zoom

Allow a trackpad pinch on the Not Sure photo stage. In side-by-side Compare, Contours, and Align, a pinch or drag affects the photo under the pointer. The toolbar shows labeled Sighting and Candidate zoom controls with independent percentages and resets.

Use a viewport for each pane and a shared viewport for Curtain and Blend. The shared viewport preserves the visual registration of the layers. Keep the existing 100–400% range and 50 percentage point button steps. Double-check that contours, pins, and AI match endpoints follow the photo they belong to.

When a different sighting side or candidate is shown, reset the changed photo's viewport and the shared viewport. Keep the two side-by-side viewports while switching stage modes.
